# 📜 Campus Eats – Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of **Campus Eats**, are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, gender, identity, experience level, nationality, or background.

We aim to foster an open and welcoming community where creativity, collaboration, and learning can thrive.

---

## Our Standards

To create a positive environment, we expect all participants to:

### ✅ Be Respectful

* Use welcoming and inclusive language.
* Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
* Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
* Show empathy toward other community members.

### ✅ Be Professional

* Communicate clearly and politely.
* Focus on what is best for the project.
* Maintain transparency in discussions and decisions.

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

* Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech.
* Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
* Trolling, spamming, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
* Publishing others’ private information without permission.
* Any form of intimidation or threatening conduct.

---

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Enforcing community standards fairly and consistently.
* Reviewing reported issues confidentially.
* Taking appropriate corrective actions when necessary.
* Promoting a healthy and productive environment.

Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject any contribution that does not align with this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

* All project spaces (GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions).
* Community interactions on social media related to Campus Eats.
* Events, meetings, and collaborations involving the project.

It applies to both online and offline interactions related to this project.

---

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

* Opening a confidential issue on GitHub (if applicable), or
* Contacting the project maintainers directly.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Violations may result in the following actions, depending on severity:

1. **Warning** – A private reminder about community standards.
2. **Temporary Ban** – Temporary restriction from project participation.
3. **Permanent Ban** – Permanent removal from the community.
4. **Content Removal** – Deletion of inappropriate content.

The maintainers will determine appropriate action based on context and impact.

---

## Community Values

We encourage contributors to:

* Help newcomers get started.
* Share knowledge and best practices.
* Collaborate openly and honestly.
* Promote ethical and responsible use of technology.

---

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by industry-standard open-source community guidelines and adapted for the Campus Eats project.

## Agreement

By participating in the Campus Eats project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a positive and supportive community.
